## Visual target — public/art/visual-target.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: polished first visual target for an original portrait mobile browser combat game, not a marketing poster. Create one portrait 2:3 image.
Primary request: rich, exceptionally crafted 1990s arcade pixel art inspired by Metal Slug's detailed mechanical sprites, expressive soldiers and chunky modern weapon designs, all original characters and assets. Crisp intentional pixel clusters, dark contour lines, warm highlights, restrained palette. No blurry painting, no vector icons, no generic flat mobile art.
Composition: slightly elevated overhead camera, near-orthographic, flat horizontally separated banks. Horizontal river through the CENTER of the image occupying about 18 percent of its height. TWO human soldiers only, one on the upper open bank facing DOWN toward the other, one on the lower open bank facing UP. They must aim across the river at each other. Soldiers visibly smaller than arena, with plenty of free space for dodging left and right and forward/back. No side-view platformer. No diagonal isometric diamond.
Environment: open weathered olive/sand riverbanks with sparse ground texture, detailed stone embankment edges, teal moving river, metal trims and light vegetation only at the outer boundaries, no obstacles in the playable banks. Center river features a raised futuristic retractable supply tower: compact circular heavy industrial cylinder, mechanical segmented panels, orange detail lights, weapon launch ports, water cascading off lower sections. Tower readable but not enormous.
Subjects: detailed human soldiers, distinctive helmets, tactical vests, backpacks, boots; one teal team and one burnt-red team. Original oversized modern assault rifles with detailed steel receivers, magazines and black grips. On each bank show one different beautifully detailed weapon pickup on the ground. Small actual muzzle flash and tracer, a small pixel blood impact, drifting smoke. No attack warning lines, target circles, hazard markings or landing markers.
UI: tasteful dark military metal health HUD at top with two health bars, small portraits; minimal readable text only 'YOU' and 'CPU'. Bottom control area contains exactly THREE distinct square weapon inventory slots with detailed rifle, grenade launcher, railgun artwork and small ammo digits; separate circular fire button at lower right. No fixed joystick. Playfield must remain visible and uncluttered.
Lighting: clear warm daylight with cool water, dramatic arcade detail but strong gameplay readability. Everything uses consistent pixel-art scale. Full-bleed game view, no phone device frame, no logos, no title, no watermark.

## Arena — public/art/arena.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: game environment background only, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Use the reference as artistic direction: detailed original Metal Slug inspired arcade pixel art with crisp pixel clusters, near-orthographic slightly overhead angle.
Create an EMPTY playable arena. Horizontal river runs across the middle from edge to edge, occupying precisely about 18 percent of the image height (from 41% to 59%). Upper and lower riverbanks are spacious open dirt and weathered concrete ground, each almost equal size. Detailed stone retaining walls border the river, teal blue water with subtle ripples. Upper bank playable area extends almost to image top; lower bank extends almost to image bottom. Outside the left and right margins only, narrow industrial boundary trims with small foliage. Sandy olive terrain, cool teal water, dark steel machinery accents.
CRITICAL: absolutely no soldiers, no characters, no weapons, no tower, no projectiles, no blood, no fire, no HUD, no health bars, no controls, no buttons, no text, no UI, no large props on the banks. The center of river must be empty water to allow separately rendered tower sprite. Ground should have subtle sparse detail and low visual noise where characters move. No blockers or cover. Not an isometric diamond, no horizon line, no perspective vanishing point. Flat full-bleed game background. Crisp pixel-art, not photorealistic, not painted.
