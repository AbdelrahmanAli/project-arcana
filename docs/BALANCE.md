# Weapon and hazard balance

These are the current 100-health, one-round playtest values. Direct-hit totals assume every shot connects; shots can miss, hit cover, or be canceled by opposing conventional rounds. Each matching pickup adds the full ammo listed below without a cap, so the per-pickup damage totals are not inventory limits.

The pistol is always available, including while carrying a Tower weapon. Both fire actions have their own cooldown; their combined damage still needs real-match playtesting.

| Weapon | Ammo | Damage | Role |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Service pistol | Unlimited | 5 per hit | Reliable fallback; 0.48-second cooldown. |
| Assault rifle | 10 rounds | 6 per hit, 60 per pickup | Accurate rapid fire; 0.18-second cooldown. |
| Tactical shotgun | 4 shells | 7 per pellet | Five pellets; typically 21 damage near the river (84 per pickup), 7 at long range. 0.8-second cooldown. |
| Stun launcher | 2 grenades | 7 center damage, 14 per pickup | 2.2-second stun and 60-unit blast radius. A hit cannot extend a stun already in progress. |
| Smart missile | 2 missiles | 28 center damage, 56 per pickup | 58-unit blast radius; captured target can move before impact. |
| Laser gun | 1 beam | 16–46 damage | Straight instant beam; needs positioning and up to 1.1 seconds to reach full charge. |
| Nuclear launcher | 2 grenades | 16 center damage, 32 per pickup | 52-unit blast radius and permanent area denial. |

On Riverside, each Tower cycle locks one firing lane across each bank during the rise and again during the descent. Each volley uses the same attack type on both banks: fire deals 20 health per second within its narrow flame strip; lightning deals one 14-damage hit within 22 units of its advancing line; icicles deal one 10-damage hit within 20 units of their advancing line. A lane is marked for 0.5 seconds before the attack travels across it. Fighters can escape with movement or a dive roll. The other maps have no Tower attacks. Nuclear waste deals 5 health per second while a fighter stands in a puddle. Overlapping waste puddles do not multiply the damage rate. Barrels remain at 22 center damage and 65-unit radius; they can hurt either fighter.

Fully broken crates independently yield nothing 55% of the time, a weapon 30% of the time, or a syringe 15% of the time. Syringes restore up to 20 health, capped at 100, and do not use the weapon slot. Random Weapons mode selects crate weapon types independently; both fixed weapon modes use their match weapon. The Nuclear Launcher remains eligible in fixed matches, and its waste stays permanently on the banks for the round.

The rifle has more potential direct damage than a missile because it requires repeated hits. The shotgun rewards advancing toward the river. The laser offers one strong, precise opportunity. Stun trades damage for control, while nuclear grenades trade immediate damage for permanent space denial. These numbers remain open to tuning after phone playtests.
