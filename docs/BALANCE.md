# Weapon and hazard balance

These are the current 100-health, one-round playtest values. Direct-hit totals assume every shot connects; shots can miss, hit cover, or be canceled by opposing conventional rounds.

| Weapon | Ammo | Damage | Role |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Service pistol | Unlimited | 5 per hit | Reliable fallback; 0.48-second cooldown. |
| Assault rifle | 10 rounds | 6 per hit, 60 per pickup | Accurate rapid fire; 0.18-second cooldown. |
| Tactical shotgun | 4 shells | 7 per pellet | Five pellets; typically 21 damage near the river (84 per pickup), 7 at long range. 0.8-second cooldown. |
| Stun launcher | 2 grenades | 7 center damage, 14 per pickup | 2.2-second stun and 60-unit blast radius. A hit cannot extend a stun already in progress. |
| Smart missile | 2 missiles | 28 center damage, 56 per pickup | 58-unit blast radius; captured target can move before impact. |
| Laser gun | 1 beam | 16–46 damage | Straight instant beam; needs positioning and up to 1.1 seconds to reach full charge. |
| Nuclear launcher | 2 grenades | 16 center damage, 32 per pickup | 52-unit blast radius and permanent area denial. |

Tower fire deals 20 health per second only while a fighter touches its narrow flame strip. Nuclear waste deals 5 health per second while a fighter stands in a puddle. Overlapping waste puddles do not multiply the damage rate. Barrels remain at 22 center damage and 65-unit radius; they can hurt either fighter.

The rifle has more potential direct damage than a missile because it requires repeated hits. The shotgun rewards advancing toward the river. The laser offers one strong, precise opportunity. Stun trades damage for control, while nuclear grenades trade immediate damage for permanent space denial. These numbers remain open to tuning after phone playtests.
