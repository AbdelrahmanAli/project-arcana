GAME SETTINGS

SETTINGS

Sound effects and controls

Drag anywhere to move. Tap DODGE while moving to roll in that direction, or roll forward when still. You cannot be hit during the roll. Its ring fills as the cooldown ends; the Tower weapon ring shrinks as ammo is spent. Matching weapon drops add a full pickup's ammo with no cap. Syringes from broken crates restore up to 20 health. The lower fire button always uses your pistol; the middle one uses your Tower weapon. Hold and release the middle button to charge the Laser Gun, up to 1.1 seconds (16–46 damage).
